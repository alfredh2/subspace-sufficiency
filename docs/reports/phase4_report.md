# Phase 4 report: where does verdict-related information become causally available to the late S8 heads?

Plan: `docs/plans/phase4_plan.md` (committed with the 12 approval amendments before any implementation). Tables: `results/phase4/report_tables.md`; mechanical verdict: `results/phase4/criteria_verdict.json`; raw per-trial arrays: `results/phase4/*.npz`. Phases 0–3 artifacts and verdicts are unchanged. No MVS optimization and no neuron or head search were run. GPU: **1.34 h of the 1.5 h cap** (`results/phase4/budget.json`).

## Preregistered outcome: **MIXED**
Residual patches at the conclusion-final token plus generation tail (G1∪G2) transfer the verdict (plateau 0.64 / 0.66 of the answer margin; both outcomes, both formats, A1 met). The transfer **starts at hook index 20**, the output of layer 19. That is *inside* the S8 layer range (19–22), so there is no separable upstream stage at residual resolution (A3: sustained onset exists in both formats but is not upstream). A2 (summary positions beat the random and non-summary controls at every index from the onset) is **not met**. The effect depends heavily on S8 outputs (P1 met), the controls are met (C1), and the nonword replication reproduces the picture.

* Pilot (validation contents, `num_orig`, 198 trials): T(All)=1.000 at all 12 indices; pre-difference patches change the margin by exactly 0.0; frozen head outputs equal the clean cached outputs (max difference 0.0); denominators above the floors (margin gap 28 logits, coordinate gap 18.4). ## Pathway dependence on S8 (Stage 3; evidence of dependence, not an exact decomposition into exclusive routes)
Patching G1∪G2 at index 20 (`num_orig` / `num_rev`), answer margin: T_total 0.62 / 0.63; **S8 frozen** 0.10 / 0.12 (D_S8 = 0.83 / 0.81, pair and content CIs within ±0.01–0.02; layer-count-matched random head sets remove 0.01); non-S8 heads in layers 19–22 frozen 0.56 / 0.58; late heads (layers ≥ 23) frozen 0.15 / 0.08; only-S8 live 0.13 / 0.08; S8 and late both frozen 0.02 / 0.01. At index 22: D_S8 = 0.83 / 0.78. For the verdict coordinate D_S8 = 0.92 at index 20 and 0.97–0.98 at index 22 (the coordinate is read at hook 23, so late-head freezing cannot affect it by construction). Freezing every last-row head gives T = 0.00 in all runs; this was not a criterion (amendment 3) and is reported descriptively. Verification that frozen heads equal their clean cached outputs held exactly (max difference 0.0) in every freeze run. Index 23 gives D_S8 = 0 exactly, as it must. The 2×2 interaction of S8-freezing and late-freezing is negative (−0.38 / −0.44 in T units): the two removals overlap, consistent with serial dependence (S8 changes feed the late heads) rather than parallel routes. D_S8 is also high at index 19 (0.74) and 17 (0.70), but T_total is only 0.15 and 0.08 there. **P1 met** (effect-size target D_S8 ≥ 0.40, above random, both formats and interval types), read as pathway dependence on S8 outputs, not as proof that S8 is the necessary or exclusive route.

## Controls (C1 met) and diagnostics
* Pre-difference positions: exact no-ops at all tested indices (difference 0.0).
* Dose-response (G1∪G2, index 20, α = 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25): 0.07, 0.19, 0.38, 0.62, 0.76 (`num_orig`; `num_rev` 0.07, 0.20, 0.39, 0.63, 0.77): monotone but convex, i.e. transfer is not linear in the patch size. Random count-matched set: 0.05, 0.13, 0.23, 0.31, 0.37.
* Norm-matched random-direction patches (same per-position norm as the true patch): T = 0.022 / 0.023 (G1∪G2), 0.03 (non-summary).
* Same-label, different-content source (set A, `num_orig`, 708 trials): at index 20 the G1∪G2 patch flips the verdict in 5.6% (mean |Δmargin| 2.2 logits); non-summary 3.5%; at index 0 the all-position same-label patch flips 8.3%, which is the lexical-content baseline of the task. Below the 0.10 limit.
* Patch strength (relative to the target's residual norm, mean over patched positions): G1∪G2 about 0.06 at index 8, 0.07 at 16, 0.115 at 20, 0.14 at 22; only about 1% of the patch energy lies along d_c. A non-summary patch is about 3× larger relative to the residual (0.38 at index 20) yet transfers less at that index, so the transfer at G1∪G2 is not a matter of patch size.

## Availability versus creation (exploratory writers, Stage 4, reduced to indices 19–20)
Patching the **attention output of layer 19** at G1∪G2 transfers 0.36 / 0.39 of the margin (G1 0.11, G2 0.18–0.19); the **MLP output of layer 19** 0.04; layer 18 attention 0.05, MLP 0.03. This identifies the attention output of layer 19 as the largest single carrier of transferable content into G1∪G2 at the onset. It does not show that layer 19 *computes* the verdict: the content may be read from premise positions that the target and source differ at, and the residual patch at index 20 (0.62) exceeds the sum of the layer-19 attention and MLP patches (0.40), so the earlier residual and these writes interact. No claim is made below the layer-19 write.

## Answer to the central question
In both numeric formats, on 8 test contents and on 43 nonword contents, verdict-related information becomes causally available at the conclusion-final token and the generation tail **jointly from hook index 20 (after layer 19)**. Neither position alone suffices (≈0.2 each). Before that, only the premise and conclusion-body positions carry it, trivially, as the lexical/structural difference. The effect on the answer then depends strongly on S8 outputs (83% removed by freezing S8's last-row outputs; random matched head sets 1%), with late heads downstream of S8. The predefined outcome is MIXED because the onset lies inside the S8 layers and because the non-summary and random controls are not low enough at the onset index.

## Limits
Availability is not creation; the pathway evidence is dependence, not exclusivity; verdict selectivity was not re-tested (Phase 3 left it unestablished); results cover the numeric logic prompts only (434 and 400 aligned trials per format); only 8 content clusters in set A. Phase 4 stops here: no upstream tracing below the layer-19 writer level.
