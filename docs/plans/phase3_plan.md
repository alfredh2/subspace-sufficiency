# Phase 3 plan — interventional mediation from the late S8 attention heads to the Phase 1 MLP units (layer 22 first)

**Standing.** New preregistered experiment. All Phase 0–2.6 artifacts, checkpoints, datasets, the frozen S8 selection, the Phase 1 unit ranking (U22 = the 278 layer-22 units of the Phase 1 top-512) and all prior verdicts (Phase 1 mixed; Phase 2 mixed; failed original recompute gate on record; Phase 2.6 localization supported) are unchanged. Outputs: `results/phase3/`. Budget ceiling **1.5 GPU-hours** (tracked in `results/phase3/budget.json`; stages stop at their gates). Not pushed; no co-author trailers. Out of scope: upstream tracing. Every conclusion concerns a **shared verdict channel, not abstract logical reasoning.**

## 0. Facts used (inspected)
Hook = output of layer 22, so `h₂₃ = h_in + attn₂₂ + mlp₂₂`. For layer-22 heads the only paths into the **residual coordinate** `c = d_c·h₂₃` are the direct attention write and MLP22 (which reads the attention output through `post_attention_layernorm`); the **final answer margin** can additionally change through layers 23–35 reading *other* residual directions. Phase 1: U22 carries +9.74 of layer-22's +10.08 net signed write. S8 heads: layer 22 {H5, H8, H15, H6, H4}, layer 21 {H19}, layer 20 {H8}, layer 19 {H10}. With last-query-row head interventions earlier positions are untouched (causality), so mediators at the answer position are the full within-layer story.

## 1. Levels of evidence and language
(i) *Accounting* (changes in activations and writes under the intervention: a readout); (ii) *causal influence* of heads on units (the head intervention is the manipulation); (iii) *interventional mediation/rescue* measures from path-specific interventions. The quantities below are **interventional rescue/transplant measures, not uniquely identified natural direct/indirect effects**; the Shapley quantity is a symmetric intervention-based attribution, **not** a causal fraction of the natural pathway. Changes in MLP activity are never taken as proof of mediation.

## 2. Design: 2×2 interventional cells
Per target example: **Y00** = clean run; **Y11** = run with the head intervention (S8_L22 etc.); **Y10** = head intervention with a unit set **U restored to its clean activations** (rescue/hold-fixed); **Y01** = clean run with U **overwritten by the activations it takes in Y11** (transplant). Outcomes Y (all reported separately; effects are signed and not assumed additive): (a) *direct attention change* — change of `d_c·attn₂₂(output)` (and per layer 19–22 for other head sets); (b) *induced U activation changes* `δa_j`; (c) *induced U verdict-directed writes* `Δs_j = g_j δa_j` and the total MLP write `d_c·mlp₂₂`; (d) the *residual coordinate* `c`; (e) the *final answer margin* (accept − reject logit) and flip rate; for ablation, the *margin class gap*.
**Aggregation (amendment).** All effects are signed shifts toward the source (patching; sign by direction) or class-gap reductions (ablation), **aggregated by averaging over trials/examples first**; ratios are formed from aggregated means, never averaged per example. `TE = ȳ11 − ȳ00`; rescue `R = (ȳ11 − ȳ10)/TE`; bypass `B = (ȳ10 − ȳ00)/TE` (`R + B = 1`); transplant `S = (ȳ01 − ȳ00)/TE`; interaction `J = R − S`; symmetric attribution `Φ = ½(R + S)`. **Paired bootstrap** (500 resamples over trials/examples, same indices for all cells) gives 95 % CIs. **Validity floors:** ratios are *undefined* if aggregated `|TE|` is below the floor (coordinate 2.0 units; margin 5.0 logits; class gap 5.0 logits), if numerical noise dominates (`|TE| < 3×` the self-restore noise floor), or if > 5 % of bootstrap replicates have `|TE_b| < ½ floor` (such replicates are discarded and counted).
**Coordinate vs margin (amendment).** Restoring *all* MLP22 activations makes the layer-22 residual coordinate change equal to the direct attention write (exact accounting identity at the hook): for the **coordinate**, `B_full` is the direct fraction. For the **final margin** the all-MLP-restored run is *not* a pure direct verdict-coordinate effect: logits still change through other residual dimensions. A supplementary **coordinate-clamp** run (the d_c coordinate at the hook reset to its clean value after the head intervention) measures how much margin effect does not pass through the verdict coordinate.

## 3. Head sets, interventions, mediator sets
* **Head sets:** primary **S8_L22** = {L22H5, H8, H15, H6, H4}; secondary L22H5 alone and S8 jointly; supplementary earlier-layer heads (L21H19, L20H8, L19H10) with mediators restricted to units at layers ≥ the head's layer. Earlier-layer head effects also flow through later heads (uncontrolled), so their `B` includes those paths.
* **Interventions:** (1) *patching* — full-`z` replacement from the opposite-class aligned partner (model-native hook; no recompute path), last query row primary, all rows secondary; aligned pairs, `num_orig` and `num_rev`, both directions. (2) *ablation* — mean replacement on the Phase 2 confirmatory class-level set (384 logic × 2 formats, localizer, PrOntoQA, MMLU-binary, Stroop, word recall); this is the only valid route for other tasks (no matched pairs needed). Patching is logic-only.
* **Mediator sets (activations at the input of `down_proj`, answer position):** primary **U22**; **U_all** (the 512 top units, layers 19–22) supplementary; the unit populations below.

## 4. Controls (taxonomy documented)
*Defined from pre-intervention data:* **random** layer-22 units (same count as U22, excluding U22; 20 draws), **activation-matched** (E|a|, E[a²] from the neutral corpus; 20 draws), **alignment-matched** (|g_j|; 20 draws), **complement** (all other layer-22 units), **full MLP22**, **dose–response** (top-k of U22 in Phase 1 rank order, k = 16, 64, 128, 278). *Selected using the intervention response (diagnostic only, not independent causal discovery):* **δ-matched** (units matched to U22 on mean induced |δa|, one draw set per head set, derived from the pilot validation pairs) and **oracle** (the 278 layer-22 units with the largest induced |Δs| on the pilot pairs). Control-matching quality is reported (standardised mean differences of the matching features); intervention-strength diagnostics: change of the MLP input norm after `post_attention_layernorm`, `Σ|δa|` and `Σ|g·δa|` over U22 vs each control, rescue per unit restored |Δs|.

## 5. Numerical correctness tests (required before any experiment)
Restoring all units reproduces the clean MLP22 output exactly; self-restore is a no-op (noise floor measured); inserting clean values into a clean run is the identity; the layer-22 accounting identity `Δc = Δ(attn write) + Δ(MLP write)`; last-row head interventions leave earlier-position activations unchanged; 2×2 algebra (`R + B = 1`, `R − S = J`); batch/padding/position alignment of cached activations; hooks are removed, `k = 0` is a no-op, control sets are deterministic (`crc32`) and disjoint from U22. **Ordering checks (amendment):** an explicit hook-order log verifies that mediator capture/restore fires **after** the affected attention output (`o_proj` pre-hook → `self_attn` output) and **before** `down_proj` of the same layer, and that mediator hooks at layer `l` are unaffected by head interventions at layers `> l` and affected by those at layers `≤ l`; verified on the tiny model and in a preflight on Qwen3 for every layer that carries an intervened head or a mediator.

## 6. Held-out status and splits
Rankings and S8 were fixed on training data. **Pilot:** aligned pairs from the *validation* contents (also used to derive the response-selected controls). **Confirmatory:** the 217 aligned test-content pairs (434 trials per format) and the Phase 2 confirmatory class-level set. The test-content pairs are held out from the MLP ranking but **were examined in the Phase 2.5/2.6 attention experiments** — they are not untouched data.

## 7. Statistics
Primary comparisons: U22 rescue vs the three pre-intervention control families (random, activation-matched, alignment-matched; 20 draws each) for coordinate and margin outcomes in both formats (12 comparisons). Reported: empirical rank of U22 among controls (`p_min = 1/21` with n = 20, not significance), z-score vs control distribution with a Shapiro check, **Holm-corrected** one-sided p-values over the 12 comparisons, and a paired-bootstrap CI of the difference. "Exceeds every draw" is necessary, not sufficient. δ-matched, oracle and complement comparisons are descriptive.

## 8. Preregistered criteria (S8_L22 and U22, patching with last-row heads, **both** numeric formats; failures reported literally)
**Validity gate:** TE above the floors and noise floor satisfied; otherwise "not evaluable".
**Mediation gate (M)** — all of: (M1) *concentration*: U22 carries ≥ 0.50 of the induced signed change in the layer-22 MLP verdict write and ≥ 3× the mean of the matched random/activation-/alignment-matched controls and exceeds all draws; (M2) *coordinate rescue* `R_c ≥ 0.40`; (M3) *margin rescue* `R_m ≥ 0.30`; (M4) U22 rescue exceeds every draw of the three control families with Holm-corrected significance and a bootstrap CI of the difference excluding 0, and the mean control rescue ≤ 0.10; (M5) *transplant* `S ≥ 0.25` (coordinate and margin).
**Generalization gate (G):** the ablation-based class-gap rescue is ≥ 0.25 on logic (both formats) and the localizer (PrOntoQA reported through raw reductions: its d_c denominator is below the floor).
**Verdict-selectivity gate (V, separate):** on the verdict-free negative controls (MMLU-binary, Stroop, word recall) the U22 rescue of the head-ablation shifts is ≤ 0.15 (or undefined because TE is below the floor) and not above the matched controls. *A substantial reproducible U22 rescue supports mediation even if unrelated tasks also show rescue; a claim of a selectively specialized verdict pathway additionally requires V.*
**Outcome table (mechanical):** M ∧ G ∧ V ⇒ *mediation supported and verdict-selective*; M ∧ ¬V (or V not evaluable) ⇒ **mixed: mediation established, specificity not established**; M with G failing ⇒ mixed (format/task-limited); partial rescue (`0.15 ≤ R_c < 0.40` or `0.10 ≤ R_m < 0.30`, one format only, `|J| > 0.20` with ordering-dependent shares, or coordinate rescued but margin not) ⇒ **mixed/partial**; `R_c ≤ 0.10` with CI upper bound < 0.20, or not above controls, or M1 ratio < 1.5 ⇒ **mediation not supported**. `B ≥ 0.70` is labelled **bypass-dominated** whatever else holds. The whole-MLP22 bound (`R_full`) is reported to show how much of the head effect can pass through MLP22 at all (units outside U22 count as alternative paths).
