**Mechanical outcome of the preregistered rule: H-generic is "supported" by family (f), "partial" by (b) and (e), "not supported" by (c), (d), (g).** Per Section 8 of the plan this means the concentration claim is downgraded before any scale-up. **Required qualification (post-hoc diagnostic, `phase7_stage0_diag.py`, not part of the rule):** the first residual PC (54 % of the variance) is itself verdict-carrying (cosine 0.75 with d_c, held-out verdict AUC 0.99, share 0.525, N_eff 50, as concentrated as d_c); the PCs 2–20 are verdict-irrelevant (cosines |cos| ≤ 0.15, AUC 0.50–0.57) and have mean held-out share 0.29 (ratio 0.60 to native, i.e. in the 'partial' band), N_eff 175–2,800. So family (f) crosses the 0.7 line only through a PC that is not behaviour-irrelevant, which is a flaw in how I defined the family; the rule is nevertheless reported as written and not re-interpreted.

**What the pilot establishes (logic only).**
1. *There is a generic floor for the top-q share in the native basis.* Any contrast/direction pair that is not pure noise yields held-out top-1 % shares of about 0.19–0.30 (shuffled labels 0.25, irrelevant partitions 0.24, verdict-irrelevant PCs 0.29, random directions 0.19), against 0.125 for Haar rotations and 0.489 for the verdict direction. The Phase 1 headline ratio (3.9 × Haar) therefore overstates how specific top-1 % share is to the behaviour; against the generic floor the factor is about 1.7–2.
2. *N_eff discriminates much better than the top-1 % share.* Native 61 (and 50 for the verdict-carrying PC1) against 175–2,800 for verdict-irrelevant PCs, 654 for shuffled labels, 1,390 for irrelevant partitions and 4,760 for random directions: the verdict write is concentrated on a few dozen units, a property the generic floor does not reproduce.
3. *Concentration of a single direction's write is not evidence of a privileged basis.* A rotation fitted to the direction's own gain vector attains the maximal sparsity analytically (one coordinate carries all of Δ_j = δ_j g_j for **any** contrast, because Δ is supported where g is), the PCA basis already reaches 0.91, and rotations fitted to other directions' gains give Haar-level concentration. Coordinate concentration of one direction measures how sparse that direction's gain vector and the class difference are in the *given* basis; it becomes informative only (i) relative to marginal-preserving baselines as above, (ii) across **many** different behavioural directions served by one fixed, unfitted basis (the multi-task design of Stages 1–4), and (iii) together with causal control and selectivity.
4. Rotations of the activation basis are a weak null on their own; native-versus-Haar ratios should not be the headline statistic.
Caveats: logic only, one hook, 50-draw families are draws from null families (not independent replications), unembedding rows were used without the final normalisation weights, the PCA basis uses a random orthonormal completion, reconstruction values for near-zero-net contrasts are not interpretable (net/abs ≈ 0.00–0.04) and are omitted from the decision.

**Preregistered rules (all four set/format combinations agree):** **M-A (magnitude artefact) TRUE** (ratio 0.84–1.04, lower bounds ≥ 0.79); **M-B (orthogonal part special) FALSE** (T(orth)/T(full) = 0.77–0.81 and random orthogonal directions ≈ 0.02–0.03 of orth, but the equal-norm d_c component is not ≤ 0.1 × orth); **M-C (position-specific gradient direction) FALSE** (T(g_eff)/T(full) ≤ 0.02).
**Interpretation.** The Phase 6 statement that the d_c-aligned component of the layer-19 write is "nearly inert" (T = 0.024) was a **magnitude effect**: scaled to the same norm, d_c transfers as much as the orthogonal remainder (and ×8 reaches the full effect), while random directions of the same norm transfer ≈ 0. Both d_c and several directions within the orthogonal complement are effective, per unit norm; the write is simply mostly (86 % of its norm) not along d_c. d_c is therefore **not** the wrong direction for this position, and the effect is **not** carried by d_c-orthogonality as such. The dose response is strongly convex (×2: 0.054, ×4: 0.143, ×8: 0.369), so natural-magnitude comparisons between components of different norm are not meaningful without norm matching. This qualifies one interpretive sentence of the Phase 6 report; the Phase 6 verdict and report are not edited. Caveats: the first-order gradient estimator is local and misses a convex, thresholded effect, so M-C tests that estimator and not every possible position-specific direction; equal-norm rescaling uses per-row factors up to 42 on rows with a small d_c component; one head set, one position, sets A and B previously examined.
**Rankings (preserved per amendment A4; 32 layer-19 heads, set A).** Geometric rankings against the causal single-head ranking (Phase 5 test A): |write on d_c| Spearman 0.29 (`num_orig`) / 0.30 (`num_rev`); |write on g_eff| 0.31 / 0.28; write norm 0.53 / 0.52; the validation causal ranking predicts the test causal ranking at 0.96 / 0.95. The top-3 by |write on d_c| and by write norm coincide with the causal top-3 {10, 31, 17}; |write on g_eff| recovers two of three. So geometric and causal rankings agree on the leading heads and disagree on the bulk (Spearman 0.3–0.5), which is itself a result: alignment to d predicts which heads matter at the top but poorly orders the remaining heads, and write norm is the better single geometric proxy here.
**Consequence under the preregistered rule:** only M-A holds ⇒ alignment to d remains the default geometric ranking; the attribution-based causal ranking and the write-norm ranking are stored alongside (A4), and the position-specific-direction ranking is not promoted.

## 3. What this changes for the plan (proposals for your review; nothing implemented)
1. **Reframe H1.** Replace the Haar ratio as headline with (i) the ratio to the generic floor (shuffled-label, irrelevant-partition and verdict-irrelevant-PC baselines) and (ii) N_eff, and define the generic-direction family as the verdict-irrelevant PCs (excluding PCs with |cos(d_c)| > 0.3 or verdict AUC > 0.7). Keep the pilot's rule as recorded: H-generic was "supported" by (f) as defined.
2. **Multi-direction fixed-basis design.** Because a fitted basis attains maximal sparsity for one direction, the privileged-basis question should be tested as: one **fixed, unfitted** basis (native units or heads) serving the writes of the verdict directions of **all five tasks** versus fitted bases adapted to a subset and evaluated on the held-out tasks. This is the quantity Stages 1–2 can measure and the pilot could not.
3. **Norm-matched causal comparisons everywhere** (Stage M's dose result: natural-magnitude component comparisons are misleading); the matched-strength requirement of A5 is retained and extended to component decompositions.
4. **Ranking panel for Stages 2–4:** d-alignment (primary, per the M-A-only outcome), attribution-based causal ranking, write-norm ranking; agreement reported.
5. Stage 1 (task feasibility and MVS sweeps, ≈ 4 GPU-h) is the next step, **not started**; it needs your go-ahead and your view on items 1–2.
