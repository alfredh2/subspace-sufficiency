## 0. Scope and declared deviations
* In S2 now: natural-strength single, pairwise and cumulative ablations with all three control families plus the on-manifold family; single, union and conditional transplants with controls; conditional co-ablation (CG) with matched controls; layerwise answer traces with final-LayerNorm and magnitude controls; out-of-sample carrier-overlap analysis; the dev-gated compensation test (B8); the same measurements at hook 21 (K<=4, 2 chains).
* **Deferred, declared:** the logic arm (3 chains, K<=4). It needs 9 new carrier fits, i.e. new carrier discovery that the S2 approval does not mention and asks to stop before. It is not run and the task contrast is not claimed.
* Controlled-strength results (dose alpha in {2,4}, norm-matched rescaling) are secondary tables, never verdict inputs (B5). They run only after the primary tables if budget allows and are labelled exploratory.
* Dev data (selection, matching, compensation candidates): Stage 1b M-letters val (13 entities) + fresh-dev (19 entities). Confirm data (all verdicts): fresh confirm, 50 entities, 400 items, 150 pairs = 300 transplant trials. The Stage 1b confirm items (reused) are used only for the intact-accuracy check.

## 1. Quantities
* gap(c) = class_gap of the four answer-letter logits (mean over class pairs); G = intact gap.
* **TE(S)** = relative necessity of ablating the union of carriers S at natural strength (h - U U^T (h - mu), all positions): (G - gap(S)) / G; **TEabs(S)** = G - gap(S) in logits.
* Single N1_k = TE({k}); joint N_J(k) = TE({1..k}); additivity excess A(S) = TE(S) - sum of singles; conditional growth CG(B|A) = TE(A u B) - TE(A). Algebraic identity (reported, not a finding): CG(B|A) - TE(B) = CG(A|B) - TE(A) = TE(A u B) - TE(A) - TE(B).
* Transplant T(S) = sum(m[patch S] - m_tgt) / sum(m_src - m_tgt) over length-matched trials, positions "all" (existing `transplant`). Conditional T(B|A) = sum(m[A ablated, patch B] - m[A ablated]) / same denominator.
* Energy fraction E(U) = mean over dev final-position residuals of ||U^T x||^2 / ||x||^2, x = h - mu (also reported over all positions). Displacement D(U) = mean over a fixed 64-item dev subset of ||h_35^abl - h_35^int|| at the answer position, h_35 = output of decoder block 35 before the final norm. Perturbation norm P(U) = mean ||U U^T x|| at the answer position.

## 2. Controls (B6)
Families for every rank 4k, k in {1,2,3,4,6,8}: (i) Haar random; (ii) on-manifold random (Q_m xi, the Phase 7 sampler); (iii) energy-matched; (iv) displacement-matched. Draws per family: 8 for k<=4, 6 for k=6,8. Haar and on-manifold draws are shared across chains. Matched families are drawn per (chain, k) from a shared pool of covariance-weighted candidates (V = Q_m diag(lambda^(a/2)) xi, a ~ U[0,3], plus flat and Haar candidates; pool of 300 candidates per k whose E and D are measured on dev only). **Matching window:** energy within +-10 % of the carrier target at the answer position; displacement within +-15 %. If fewer than the required draws fall inside the window the closest draws are taken and the family is flagged `unmatched` with the achieved ratios; nothing is rescaled. For k=1 the target is the mean over the chain's eight carriers. A joint effect exceeds controls only if it exceeds the **maximum over all draws of all four families** (the stricter reading of B6). Conditional co-ablation controls: for each ordered pair (A,B) among the first four carriers, rank-4 subspaces orthogonal to A drawn Haar-in-complement, energy-matched to B, displacement-matched to B (6 draws each); control CG = TE(A u R) - TE(A); the on-manifold family is dropped there (declared).

## 3. Criteria, mechanically
Per-chain scalar x for each criterion, expressed as a margin (x >= 0 means the chain meets it). Support/median/bound logic is the Stage 1b logic: PASS = support rule met and the hierarchical 95 % bound (resample chains, then entity clusters; 400 resamples; entity clusters resampled jointly for ablation items and transplant trials) on the passing side; FAIL = support fails and the median is on the failing side; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. Seed support per B2: necessity-type >= 3 of 5, sufficiency/transplant-type >= 4 of 5.
* **MSR**: from S1, unchanged (PASS on dev and confirm). Not re-scored.
* **R1** (single necessity absent; necessity-type): chain value = median over the eight carriers of TE({k}). Margin x = 0.10 - value. The bound uses the 0.15 threshold on the upper bound of the median over chains. Also required: Haar rank-4 control TE mean <= 0.05 and every effect above 2x the batch noise floor (0.132 logits, i.e. 0.0025 relative); otherwise R1 is INCONCLUSIVE.
* **R2** (joint necessity): for each k in {2,3,4,6,8}: m1 = TE(1..k) - 0.30; m2 = (TEabs - 10) / G; m3 = TE(1..k) - maxctrl_k - 0.15, maxctrl_k the max over all draws of all four families at k. x_R2 = max_k min(m1, m2, m3).
* **R3** (super-additivity): x_JCD = max_k min(m1, m2, m3, A(1..k) - 0.15), same k as R2. **JCD** = verdict of x_JCD (it contains R2 and R3).
* **R4** (union transplant; transplant-type, >= 4 of 5): for k in {2,4,8}: x = min(T(1..k) - 0.25, 0.15 - max_{i<=k} T({i}), 0.05 - maxctrl T_k), maxctrl over all draws of the four families at rank 4k; x_R4 = max_k.
* **R5** (multi-carrier dependence; necessity-type): over the six pairs among carriers 1-4: x = min(TE(AuB) - TE(A) - TE(B) - 0.10, CG(B|A) - maxctrl(B|A), CG(A|B) - maxctrl(A|B)); x_R5 = max over pairs.
* **SCR** = JCD and R1 and R4 and R5 all PASS. JCD without one of them is reported as "JCD only". **ASR** = N_J(8) < 0.10 and T(1..8) < 0.15 in >= 3 of 5 chains. Anything else is MIXED, or INCONCLUSIVE if a needed criterion is INCONCLUSIVE and none is FAIL.
* **SEMANTIC-DUPLICATE** is not re-asserted from the letter probe (non-discriminative at hook 25). S2 reports ablation/transplant effect vectors on the four answer logits, the pairwise cosine of effect vectors, and the out-of-sample coordinate-predictability analysis below; the label is applied only when two carriers share the same effect profile (cosine >= 0.5 over items with |effect| above the noise floor) AND each coordinate set is predicted out of sample by the other with per-dimension R^2 >= 0.5 above the random-subspace baseline R^2. Descriptive.
* **Hook robustness / readout flag**: as section 9 of the plan.

## 4. Out-of-sample carrier overlap
Fit on dev (Stage 1b train subsample of 600 + val + fresh-dev items), evaluate on the fresh confirm items; ridge (alpha 1) predictive R^2 reported variance-weighted and as the per-dimension mean, with entity-cluster bootstrap CIs; held-out canonical correlations (weights fitted on dev, correlations measured on confirm); baselines: predictor coordinates from random 4-d subspaces, and a class-only predictor (answer-letter one-hot). Reported pooled over carrier pairs per chain.

## 5. Layerwise traces and passive-repair controls (B11)
Logit-lens margins (final norm + the four letter unembedding rows) of the residual after blocks 26, 27, 29, 31, 33, 35 and the real logits for: intact, A1-ablated, U1:4 and U1:8 ablated, and the controls at rank 16 and 32 (energy- and displacement-matched, first 4 draws). Final-LayerNorm scale frozen to the intact value is applied as a recomputed lens ("ln-frozen"); the intervention-magnitude controls are the matched families. Dip-then-recover profiles are candidate signatures only.

## 6. Compensation (B8), gated on dev
* Components: attention output (o_proj) and MLP output (down_proj) of blocks 25-35 at the answer position (22 components). DLA of component c on the correct-minus-others margin uses the intact final-norm scale (frozen) so the nominations are not LN-rescaling artefacts.
* **Gate C1' (dev only):** the set C of n = 6 components with the largest conditional-importance growth CIG, where CIG(c) = |effect of ablating component c on the margin| in the U1:4-ablated run minus the same in the intact run, effects by zero-ablating c's output at the answer position relative to the run's own value; selected on dev, must exceed the maximum of the same statistic over the matched control ablations (energy and displacement matched, rank 16) and persist with the final LN scale frozen. If no set passes, the block is not run and ACT is "not detected at component granularity".
* **Block test C2 (confirm):** pin C at the answer position to intact-run values in the ablated run (`pinned_rows`); BE as in B8; controls: 20 random size-6 component sets, pins under matched controls, pin of C in the intact run (must be 0). ACT needs C1' and C2 (>= 3 of 5 chains, necessity-type). A rise on pinning is reported as passive propagation. Because natural-strength joint ablations may leave behaviour intact (nothing to compensate) or break it (nothing preserved), ACT is only scored in chains where the dev U1:4 ablation preserves >= 50 % of the gap (preserved-performance condition); otherwise the chain is "not applicable".

## 7. Addendum S2-b (compensation operationalisation; committed before the compensation dev gate or any confirm-set compensation run)
* Margin for all compensation quantities: m_i = logit(gold letter) - mean of the other three letter logits, computed in fp32 from the block-35 output h_L at the answer position as ((h_L / rms) * g_final) @ W_letters^T. rms is the run's own (unfrozen) or the reference run's (frozen). G_m = mean intact margin; "relative" = divided by G_m; "logits" = absolute.
* Zero-ablation effect of component c in a run X: E_X(c) = m(X) - m(X with c's output at the answer position set to 0). CIG(c) = (|E_abl(c)| - |E_int(c)|)/G_m with X = U1:4 ablated versus intact. Set statistic S(C|X) = (|E_X(C)| - |E_int(C)|)/G_m with C zeroed jointly. Candidate set C = the six components with the largest CIG on dev (pooled dev items), per chain.
* Gate C1' (dev): S(C|U1:4) >= 0.10 AND > max over {20 random size-6 component sets (same attn/MLP counts as C, blocks 25-35)} of S(set|U1:4) AND > max over the matched controls (energy and displacement matched, rank 16, 4 draws each, and Haar) of S(C|control) AND the same three comparisons hold with the final-norm scale frozen to the reference run. Chains failing the gate are "ACT not detected at component granularity".
* Preserved-performance condition (dev): TE(U1:4) <= 0.50; otherwise the chain is "ACT not applicable".
* Block test C2 (confirm): BE(C) = [TE of (U1:4 ablation with C pinned to intact values at the answer position)] - TE(U1:4 ablation), in relative gap units, plus the absolute logits. Pass per chain: BE >= 0.10 and BE_abs >= 5 logits and chain lower bound > 0.05 (entity-cluster bootstrap), mean over 20 random size-6 sets |BE| <= 0.03, BE under every matched carrier control (energy, displacement, 4 draws) < BE(C) (compensation-specific), and the pin of C in the intact run reproduces the intact margin (|BE| < 1e-6 relative). ACT = C1' passes and C2 passes in >= 3 of 5 applicable chains (necessity-type); C1' passes and C2 fails = "response without causal contribution (FAIL)"; a negative BE (pinning increases performance) = passive propagation.
