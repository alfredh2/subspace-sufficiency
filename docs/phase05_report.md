# Phase 0.5 report — logic inference vs. verdict/answer-token mechanisms

Setting: frozen Qwen3-8B, the three Phase-0 rank-2 MVS checkpoints (seeds 0/1/2, `residual_index=23`, step 1600), same benchmark (768 logic examples = Items+Families+Nonwords; re-created benchmark, see `paper_reproduction.md`), same hook/convention. **Nothing was retrained or tuned.** Full tables: `results/phase05/report_tables.md`; raw per-example arrays: `results/phase05/*.npz`; code: `scripts/phase05_*.py`, `src/causal_subspaces/phase05.py`, hook `residual_patch` in `models/hooks.py`; tests: `tests/test_phase05.py` (48 tests pass in total).
Faithful-vs-approximate: Phase-0 items are unchanged. New in this phase and **not from the paper**: the four alternative label formats, the matched-pair patching benchmark, all diagnostics.

## Summary of findings
1. **Sufficiency (retention) is format-bound, not semantic.** Retained MVS reproduces the trained `valid→"1", invalid→"2"` mapping and nothing else: under the reversed mapping (1=invalid) accuracy falls to 0.04 (AUC 0.01 — systematically inverted); with A/B or Yes/No instructions the retained model still emits "1"/"2" (100% invalid-format outputs). Intact accuracy is 0.954–0.958 in all five formats, so these failures are properties of the retained model, not of the prompts.
2. **Ablation adds a verdict-level "valid/accept" bias and compresses, but does not erase, example-dependent information.** In seeds 0 and 2 the ablated model answers constant "valid" (acc 0.500, P(valid)=1.00 in `num_orig`); under reversed mapping it answers the *semantic* "valid" verdict (seed 2: all "2", all "B"), not the token "1" (seed 0 partly: P(valid)=0.84/0.90). Yet the valid-vs-invalid logit margin still separates the classes: AUC 0.95–0.99 in every format and seed, best-single-threshold accuracy 0.90–0.96. The effect is a large rise of logit("1") (+8) *and* fall of logit("2") (−6.5) plus a 3–4× smaller class gap (28.5 → 6–8 logits for seeds 0/2), i.e. a combination of global shift, suppression of the reject answer and loss of gain.
3. **The effect is not logic-specific.** The same collapse (constant "1" with preserved ranking, AUC 1.000) occurs on the language localizer (gold 1=English, 2=nonsense; acc 1.0 → 0.562 in all seeds), and on PrOntoQA in seeds 0/2 (0.758 → 0.46/0.45; seed 1 0.648, non-constant). Verdict-free tasks that use the same label tokens (MMLU-binary, Stroop, word recall) are unaffected by ablation and receive **zero** transfer from MVS patching (transfer ≈ 0.00–0.01).
4. **One direction, shared across seeds, carries the damage.** Principal-angle cosines between seeds are ≈0.77–0.83 for one direction and 0.06–0.19 for the other. Ablating only the cross-seed-consensus rank-1 direction reproduces the full constant-"1" collapse (logic 0.500; localizer 0.562; PrOntoQA 0.438); ablating the seed-specific complement leaves everything intact (logic 0.94–0.98, localizer 1.00). Seed 1's shared direction gives partial damage (0.75/0.75/0.65), consistent with its weaker rank-2 effect.
5. **Counterfactual patching shows the MVS carries a verdict-like signal that can be transplanted:** on 448 matched valid/invalid pairs (same sentences, fact/conclusion roles swapped) replacing only the MVS coordinates moves the answer toward the source (transfer 0.49–0.64, flip rate 50–69% of clean trials, all positions) while 8 random rank-2 subspaces give |transfer| ≤ 0.002 and the seed-specific direction ≈ 0. Lexical same-label controls change the verdict in only 3–4% of trials (|Δmargin| ≈1.0–1.5 vs. shifts of 10–20 for real pairs).

## What the MVS is sufficient to preserve
* The *trained input→output mapping* on the 1/2 prompt: retention keeps accuracy at 0.941–0.949 / 0.977–0.980 / 0.949 and the 1-vs-2 margin AUC at 0.992–0.994 (= intact), for all seeds. This was the optimisation target (KL on that single prompt format) so success is expected.
* It does **not** preserve instruction-conditional behaviour: the label→verdict mapping given in the prompt (reversed/A-B/Yes-No) is lost, because it lives outside the 2 retained dimensions (everything else is replaced by the corpus mean). Under A/B and Yes/No the margin between the *requested* tokens retains partial ranking information for some seeds (AUC 0.78–0.99 for A/B; 0.27–0.97 for Yes/No) but the argmax token is off-format. Therefore "retained logic accuracy" in Phase 0 should be read as "retained 1/2 verdict readout", not "retained abstract logic".
* Rank-1 retention of the shared direction: the 1-vs-2 margin still discriminates (AUC 0.993) but the model stops emitting "1"/"2" (accuracy 0, WikiText KL ≈7.4), so rank-1 sufficiency cannot be assessed by accuracy; this is reported as a negative result. Rank-1 retention of seed-specific directions yields a constant "1" with AUC 0.42–0.93.

## What its ablation causally disrupts
* The mapping from internal evidence to the *affirmative-vs-rejecting verdict* on verdict-structured tasks (logic validity, English-vs-nonsense, true/false deduction). Disruption = shift toward the affirmative answer + reduced separation, with ranking largely intact.
* It does not disrupt tasks without an accept/reject verdict, nor language modelling (WikiText KL 0.001–0.006 for rank-1 interventions).

## Semantic or answer-format-specific?
Mixed, with clear evidence on both sides, so neither label is accurate alone:
* *Semantic/verdict-level:* ablated seeds follow the instruction-mapped "valid" label under reversal, A/B and Yes/No (seed 2: P(valid)=1.00/1.00/0.95); patching in the reversed format has positive semantic transfer (0.27–0.43 all-position, vs 0.49–0.64 in the original format).
* *Format-specific:* retention is bound to the literal 1→valid mapping; patching transfer drops by roughly a third to a half under reversal and for last-position patches; seed 0's ablation has a literal-token component (P(lit-first) 0.16 under reversal vs P(valid) 0.84).
* *Not logic-specific:* identical signatures on the language localizer and (partly) PrOntoQA. The data cannot separate "abstract logic" from "generic accept/reject judgement" for this subspace; the matched-pair patching only shows the MVS carries *a verdict-like variable correlated with validity in these pairs*. A prediction flip is not evidence of abstract logical content: the pairs share all sentences but differ in role assignment, which could also alter other surface-level properties.

## Same direction across seeds?
Yes for the dominant shared direction (consensus↔each seed's shared direction cosine 0.91–0.93; shared↔shared 0.76–0.82); its ablation collapses seeds 0 and 2 completely and seed 1 partially; the orthogonal seed-specific direction is inert in ablation and patching in all seeds (specific↔specific cosine −0.06–0.19, i.e. unrelated across seeds). Seed 1 is the exception in strength, not in kind.

## Suitability for native-neuron studies of abstract logic
* The **rank-1 consensus (shared) direction** is the stable, causally relevant object: it is reproducible across seeds, necessary for the collapse, and transplantable. It is suitable for asking "which native neurons write this direction", but what it encodes is best described as a **validity-correlated accept/reject verdict signal**, not abstract logic. Claims such as "neurons implementing logic" would not be supported by it.
* The **rank-2 seed-specific complement** carries no detected function in these tests and should be dropped from neuron analyses.
* Before Phase 1: if the aim is logic *specifically*, a subspace should be sought that separates validity from generic accept/reject (e.g. contrast logic-vs-localizer/PrOntoQA verdict conditions, or require preservation of logic discrimination after removing the shared verdict direction). Not implemented here.

## Caveats and negative findings
* 3 MVS seeds, a re-created benchmark, a single layer/hook convention (23); control item counts are small (128 per control).
* Patching pairs differ in role assignment (fact vs conclusion), not by a single-token logical operator; "tightly matched" means identical sentence content and equal token length (mean 7.0 differing token positions), not minimal edit distance. Positions are aligned only in the sense of equal prompt length.
* "Transfer" is a ratio of means of logit margins, which saturate; flip rates are over clean trials only. Last-position-only patches are weaker than all-position patches, as expected for a multi-position computation.
* The localizer's last-position patch transfers asymmetrically (toward-"English" 0.56–0.69, toward-"nonsense" 0.08–0.14) and the complement (non-MVS) patch transfers as much as the MVS there, unlike on logic (MVS 0.36–0.49 vs complement 0.16–0.27).
* Yes/No was run with a single natural mapping (Yes=valid); a reversed mapping is not natural.
* The earlier Phase-0 statement "ablation drops logic to chance" is re-characterised: the ablated model's *discrimination* is mostly intact (AUC ≥0.95); its *verdict calibration* is destroyed.
